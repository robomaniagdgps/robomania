Introduction

Welcome to Robomania 2026

Robation is organised by GD Goenka School. It is an inter-school event held annually with its inaugural session being held in 2026.

Robation encourages innovation, teamwork, and hands-on application of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Participants test their creativity and problem-solving skills in a competitive yet collaborative environment.

Our Objectives

  1. 🎯

    To offer opportunities to students to showcase their inner engineer

  2. 📚

    To provide a platform for learning outside of books

  3. 🚀

    To connect learning to future STEM careers

  4. 💡

    To enhance their creativity and leadership skills
